## 1. Business outcome and KPI

## 2. Trigger event(s)

## 3. Required data and source systems

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Plain code, no model (rule 6). Checks run on the claim snapshot at release time:

| Rule | Fails when | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| `required_fields` | no service date, lines or diagnosis, or a line lacks a CPT or diagnosis pointer | fix task (billing) |
| `duplicate_claim` | an earlier claim (known before this one) has the same patient, payer, date of service and codes | hold review (billing) |
| `timely_filing` | fewer than 30 days left of the payer's filing limit (365 days when the payer has no `payer_filing_window` row) | hold review (billing) |
| `ncci_pair` | a pair in `ncci_pair` on one date: indicator 0 always, indicator 1 when no modifier is present | hold review (coding) |
| `mue_units` | units of one code on one date exceed `mue_limit` | fix task (coding) |

Not evaluated (reported on every record, not guessed): eligibility on date of service and authorization units; the repo has no 270/271 or authorization data.
**The NCCI and MUE rows are synthetic stand-ins, not the CMS tables**, and the 30-day warning and 365-day default are placeholders for sign-off.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

Predicting denial reasons and simulating adjudication: rules plus a calibrated, interpretable model, falling back to pooled payer-family priors where volume is thin, evaluated on time-based splits (strategy Section 4). No model is approved yet; the engine's stub provider is the default and a real provider needs an approved BAA (strategy Decision 6).

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

Runs payer-specific and practice-specific validation, predicts denial reasons, simulates likely adjudication, recommends/fixes high-confidence defects before submission. Build-plan checks: required fields, NCCI pair without modifier, MUE, auth units, eligibility on DOS, duplicate, timely-filing days. Flags only; never edits codes. Action types built: `a5_fix_task` and `a5_hold_review`, both tasks for a named human; no action edits a claim or submits it. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

Gold-standard labels are double-reviewed, agreement is measured, disagreements are adjudicated by the RCM domain lead, and the labeling guideline is versioned (strategy 11B).
