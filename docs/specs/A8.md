## Purpose
For every denial, takeback or recoupment on an 835: decide **who owns it, what kind of fix it needs, by when, and what it is likely
worth**, and record that decision. A8 only proposes tasks and reviews for a named human. It never appeals, resubmits, corrects a code,
writes off or refunds (rules 3 and 4).

## Triggers (`agent.trigger_kind`, decided from the event payload)
| trigger | event | condition |
|---|---|---|
| `denial` | `835.remit` | CLP02 = 4 |
| `denial_line` | `835.remit` | a service line paid 0 with a non-patient adjustment other than CARC 45 |
| `takeback` | `835.remit` | CLP02 = 22 (reversal) |
| `recoupment` | `835.payment` | a PLB adjustment with reason `WO` or `72` (config in the agent) |

## Inputs
The remit / payment rows of the trigger event, its claim, CAS adjustments, the tenant's `carc_route` and `payer_appeal_window`
tables, fee schedule and earlier remits (for A15 pricing and the payer + CARC win history). Only data known at the event's time
is used. Note: a remit's link to its claim is written by `krisnai match` after the fact, so run matching before replaying.

## Deterministic rules (step 3, recorded on every decision)
`claim_matched`, `routing_found` (the CARC has a `carc_route` row), `appeal_window_from_table` (else the 180-day default),
`deadline_open`, `amount_priced` (A15 fee-schedule pricing, else billed charge `billed_fallback`).

## Routing tables (config, not code; seeded by `krisnai a8 seed`, editable per tenant)
| CARC | category | owner | disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27, 31 | coverage | front desk | correctable |
| 22 | coordination of benefits | front desk | correctable |
| 197, 198 | authorization | front desk | appealable |
| 4, 11 | coding | coding | correctable |
| 97 | bundling | coding | appealable |
| 50 | medical necessity | coding | appealable |
| 16 | missing information | billing | correctable |
| 18 | duplicate | billing | write-off review |
| 29 | timely filing | billing | write-off review |
A CARC with no row is routed to billing as write-off review and flagged `unrouted`. `payer_appeal_window` holds days per payer (default 180)
counted from the payment date. All of this is a default for sign-off.

## Model use
Only for appealable denials: a draft appeal built from payer code, CARC, CPT codes, amount and dates (no names, member ids or birth
dates). The provider is the stub; its text is stored as `appeal_draft` and the prompt hash is on the decision record.

## Outputs and actions (each is a task or review for a human)
| action type | when | owner |
|---|---|---|
| `a8_fix_task` | correctable denial | by CARC |
| `a8_appeal_task` | appealable denial | by CARC |
| `a8_write_off_review` | write-off review or unrouted | billing |
| `a8_takeback_review` | CLP02 22 | billing |
| `a8_recoupment_review` | PLB recoupment | billing |
Payload: trigger, payer, claim, category, owner, disposition, CARCs, CPTs, deadline, amount at risk, pricing basis, win probability and basis,
expected recovery, unrouted flag, appeal draft. Win probability = (wins + 1) / (n + 2) over earlier denials of the same payer + CARC whose
claim was later paid, known at decision time. Expected recovery = amount at risk x win probability (takebacks and recoupments: probability 1).

## Policy and autonomy
No policy row = allowed, L0, shadow (record only). Nothing in A8 is above L0 until a tenant's `policy` rows are changed with evidence.
Even a live L4 policy cannot make a replay act: replay is always shadow with no adapters.

## Guardrails
Rules 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10 of CLAUDE.md. Idempotent on (tenant, event, agent, action). Cross-tenant: each tenant reads only its own routing tables.
