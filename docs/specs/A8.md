- **Phase:** MVP; **release:** Wave 1 (A5, A6, A7, A8, A14); **milestone:** M7
- **Build status:** Built in shadow mode (M7): docs/specs/A8.md replaces the earlier version. Replay on synthetic: 23 decisions, 0 executed.
- **Spec status:** Draft on the strategy's Appendix A (24 fields). Fields the strategy does not state are marked TBD for the owners named in the strategy; thresholds stay TBD until fixed from baseline data before shadow results are reviewed.
- **Engine support (built, M6):** policy table (default allowed, L0, shadow), decision records, idempotency, point-in-time replay, approval queue service, disabled/stub adapters.

## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Denial overturn rate; recovery $; cycle time.

## 2. Trigger event(s)

835 denial (CLP02 4 or a denied service line), takeback (CLP02 22) or recoupment (PLB reason WO or 72).

## 3. Required data and source systems

835 remits and PLB, claims, CAS adjustments, fee schedule (for A15 pricing), earlier remits for the payer + CARC win history; tenant tables carc_route and payer_appeal_window. Appeal evidence gathering is not built.

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Routing and appeal windows are tenant tables, not code. Defaults awaiting sign-off: CARC 27, 31 and 22 coverage (front desk, correctable); 197 and 198 authorization (front desk, appealable); 4 and 11 coding (coding, correctable); 97 bundling and 50 medical necessity (coding, appealable); 16 missing information (billing, correctable); 18 duplicate and 29 timely filing (billing, write-off review). A CARC with no row routes to billing as write-off review and is flagged unrouted. Recoupment PLB reasons WO and 72 are a default in the agent. Win probability = (wins + 1) / (n + 2) over earlier denials of the same payer + CARC whose claim was later paid and known at decision time.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

Appeal drafting for appealable denials, from payer code, CARC, CPT codes, amount and dates only (no names, member ids or birth dates). Provider: the stub. No model is approved yet; the engine's stub provider is the default and a real provider needs an approved BAA (strategy Decision 6).

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

Built: classifies root cause by CARC routing table, identifies the owner (front desk, coding, billing), calculates the deadline (payment date + payer appeal window, default 180 days) and expected recovery (amount at risk x smoothed payer+CARC win rate), drafts an appeal with the stub model. Proposes only: a8_fix_task, a8_appeal_task, a8_write_off_review, a8_takeback_review, a8_recoupment_review. Not built: gathering supporting evidence, tracking outcomes. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

## 18. Point-in-time replay method and label double-review process

The engine's replay harness (built, M6) runs the agent over history using only data known at each event's time; later corrections are excluded or flagged. Gold-standard labels are double-reviewed, agreement is measured, disagreements are adjudicated by the RCM domain lead, and the labeling guideline is versioned (strategy 11B). Gold set and guideline: TBD.

## 19. Action reversibility class (reversible, compensable or irreversible) and rollback or compensation design
