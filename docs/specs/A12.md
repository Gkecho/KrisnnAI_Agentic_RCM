# A12 Patient Financial Engagement Agent

- **Phase:** Phase 2; **release:** After the 180-day plan; **milestone:** M19
- **Build status:** Built in M19 as a **staff-facing worklist, shadow mode only** (`krisnai/agents/a12_patient/`, `krisnai a12 sweep|replay`). Reads two new feeds, patient payments (`patient_payment`) and statement / account status (`patient_statement`), migration 0014. **No outreach of any kind is built.**
- **Spec status:** Draft on the strategy's Appendix A (24 fields). Fields the strategy does not state are marked TBD for the owners named in the strategy; thresholds stay TBD until fixed from baseline data before shadow results are reviewed.
- **Engine support (built, M6):** policy table (default allowed, L0, shadow), decision records, idempotency, point-in-time replay, approval queue service, disabled/stub adapters.

## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Patient collections; days patient AR.

## 2. Trigger event(s)

Patient responsibility confirmed, or a balance ages.

## 3. Required data and source systems

Patient balances, statements, contact preferences. Not in the repo yet.

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Plain code in `rules.check`, one claim's patient share (835 CLP05 of the latest non-reversed, non-denied remit) less patient payments, as of a date. Read in this
order, as a person would: a dispute or hardship status stops everything (`dispute_hold`); an overpayment is a `credit_balance`; a payment plan is not chased; an open
balance under 10.00 is a `small_balance` (a staff decision); no statement 7 days after the share was confirmed is `no_statement`; an open balance with a statement sent and
30 / 60 / 90 days since confirmation is `balance_aging` with that stage. Placeholders for sign-off. The explanation on each card is a template from the PR reason codes
(deductible, coinsurance, copay): no model, no identifiers, text for staff only.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

Generating personalized explanations. No model is approved yet; the engine's stub provider is the default and a real provider needs an approved BAA (strategy Decision 6).

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

Generates a personalized statement/explanation, selects channel and timing, detects payment-plan eligibility and disputes, creates a staff task for sensitive cases. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

## 7. Confidence/materiality thresholds

Thresholds are set per action type from the signed-off baseline and shadow evidence, not stated here (strategy Section 6): TBD at spec review.

## 8. Human approval role and SLA

## 13. Shadow-mode evaluation dataset and acceptance criteria

Dataset and acceptance criteria are fixed before shadow results are reviewed: TBD at spec review.

**Compliance boundary (built in, not configurable):** the agent has no outbound channel. No statement, text, call, email, portal link or payment request is created or
sent; no action can refund, write off, collect or adjust a balance (a test asserts the action names). Every card says it is staff-facing. Outreach needs, before it is
considered: legal review of TCPA consent, Reg F contact rules and state collection law; a consent source; an approved messaging vendor under a BAA; payment processing.

**Synthea mini book (1,200 claims, `--patient`):** every planted outcome found with its dollars and nothing else (dispute holds, credits, small balances, missing
statements, aging by stage); collections and open balance consistent with the answer key; 0 executed. Finding on the way: the first answer key only described claims
older than 45 days, so five small open balances on younger claims looked like false positives; the agent was right and the key was incomplete. The feeds and outcomes
are simulated by the same team that wrote the rules.

Gold-standard labels are double-reviewed, agreement is measured, disagreements are adjudicated by the RCM domain lead, and the labeling guideline is versioned (strategy 11B).
