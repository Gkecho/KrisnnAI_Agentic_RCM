# A7 AR Next-Best-Action Agent

- **Phase:** MVP; **release:** Wave 1 (A5, A6, A7, A8, A14); **milestone:** M9
- **Build status:** Built in M9, shadow mode only (`krisnai/agents/a7_ar/`, `krisnai a7 sweep|replay|inbox`). No migration: no new tables.
- **Spec status:** Draft on the strategy's Appendix A (24 fields). Fields the strategy does not state are marked TBD for the owners named in the strategy; thresholds stay TBD until fixed from baseline data before shadow results are reviewed.
- **Engine support (built, M6):** policy table (default allowed, L0, shadow), decision records, idempotency, point-in-time replay, approval queue service, disabled/stub adapters.

## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Days in AR; AR > 90; touches per claim (days in AR and AR > 90 are defined in docs/metrics.md).

## 2. Trigger event(s)

Claim unpaid beyond its expected payment date or a risk threshold.

## 3. Required data and source systems

Claims, 276/277 status, payer history, timely-filing limits, balances (strategy Section 10).

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Plain code, no model (rule 6). A **sweeper** raises one `ar.followup_due` event per open claim once it passes its payer's expected
payment date (the engine only runs on events), and again every 14 days while it stays open. The agent then checks:

| Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `claim_open` | no payer answer (835) known by the event time; answered claims are A8/A10/A12's, not A7's |
| `past_expected_date` | age since submission is at least the payer's usual days to pay |
| `acknowledged` | a 277CA is on file |
| `amount_priced` | the unpaid amount came from the fee schedule (A15 pricing), not the billed fallback |
| `history_sufficient` | the payer has at least 5 earlier paid claims, else a 30-day default is used |

Expected days to pay = the payer's median over earlier paid claims (point in time). Chance a claim pays on its own = (paid + 1) / (n + 2) over
that payer's claims at least 90 days old. **Priority = unpaid amount x (1 - chance it pays on its own) x urgency**; urgency = 1 + days past
expected / expected days (capped at +2), plus 1 when fewer than 60 days remain before the payer's filing limit (A5's `payer_filing_window`).
The 5-claim minimum, 30-day default, 90-day maturity, 14-day repeat, 60-day filing-risk window and 90-day escalation age are placeholders for sign-off.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

Estimating payment/recovery probability. No model is approved yet; the engine's stub provider is the default and a real provider needs an approved BAA (strategy Decision 6).

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

**Built actions** (tasks and reviews for a person; none resubmits, writes off, refunds or edits a code): `a7_status_check_task` (billing), `a7_resubmit_review`
(claim returned by the 277CA; billing), `a7_escalate_review` (no answer for 90+ days; A/R lead). Every proposal carries an **inbox card**: title, why (plain
language), priority, expected value, deadline, owner, next steps and the rule evidence; `krisnai a7 inbox` prints the ranked cards, latest per claim,
leaving out claims the payer has since answered. Not built: real 276/277 status inquiry (no parser or data; belongs with A6's clearinghouse adapter),
detecting payer requests for records, and routing to registration or authorization (see `docs/RCM_REVIEW.md`).

Interprets 276/277 and payer history, estimates payment/recovery probability, ranks work by dollars x probability x deadline, recommends or executes the next step. Build plan: expected payment date per payer; priority = $ x (1 - p_paid) x urgency. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.
