## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Net collection rate; recovered leakage; A15-lite: validated leakage baseline delivered.

## 2. Trigger event(s)

Unexpected variance in collections, payer mix or service-line yield. A15-lite is run on request as a read-only diagnostic on historical data. The full agent's trigger is the sweeper's `revenue.variance` event: on each sweep date (default every 28 days) one event per payer or payer+CPT slice whose realisation against contract fell, again when the fall or the top cause changes, and once when it clears.

## 3. Required data and source systems

Historical 837/835 and ATB plus PM data (visits extract). A15-lite runs on claims, remits, fee schedule and visits.

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

**Built (M21), plain code, no model.** Per remit line (matched, not reversed, priced from the fee schedule): expected = the contract allowed amount; actual = paid plus the patient's share (a denied line pays nothing); realisation = actual / expected. A slice is a payer, or a payer and a CPT. It is flagged when realisation over the lines paid in the **recent window (56 days)** fell **5 points or more** below the **baseline window (the 180 days before it)**, with at least **8 lines** in each window and **500.00** of contract value in the recent window. Dollar impact = fall x recent expected. All of these are placeholders for the revenue assurance lead.

Each shortfall line in the recent window is explained by the A15-lite finding on its claim: underpayment on that line, else the claim's denial (by category and CARC), else an unexplained adjustment, else **unexplained** (reported, never guessed). The upstream table tags each line's claim with the agents (A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A13) whose decision record flagged it **at or before the day the claim became known**; claims nobody flagged are counted under `none`. That is the root-cause graph: slice -> cause -> upstream. It is attribution by key match to leaks already detected, not a proof of cause.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

Explains variance by payer/CPT/location/provider, traces lost revenue to root causes, estimates recoverable vs structural leakage and creates an investigation plan. **Built action (M21): one, `a15_investigation_review`, owned by the revenue assurance lead**, carrying the slice, the fall, the cause table and the upstream table; a card, nothing executed. Location and provider slices are not built. A15-lite (built) detects denials, timely-filing losses, underpayments, rejections, unbilled visits and unexplained adjustments, splits recoverable from structural, maps each to the agent that would work it, and stops if data does not reconcile (G0). Details: docs/specs/a15_lite.md. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

## 13. Shadow-mode evaluation dataset and acceptance criteria

Dataset and acceptance criteria are fixed before shadow results are reviewed: TBD at spec review.

**Real Synthea book (2026-10-15, 300 patients, seed 7, `--documentation --eligibility`, `--rate-cut SYNANTHEM:99213:2026-08-01:30`, `--denial-pattern SYNHUMANA:99214:50:0.6:2026-08-01`), sweep every 28 days from 2026-04-14:**
- The planted rate cut is flagged as slice `SYNANTHEM|99213` with top cause underpayment: 10 lines, **329.00**, exactly the planted underpayments paid in the window.
- The planted denial pattern is flagged as `SYNHUMANA|99214` with top cause `denial:medical_necessity`: **2 lines**, exactly the 2 planted claims paid in the window.
- Three other slices were also flagged at the last sweep (`SYNBLUECROSS`, `SYNMEDICARE|99213`, `SYNMEDICARE|99214`), plus the `SYNHUMANA` payer total that contains the planted slice. They are random denials and timely-filing losses in small samples (the 8-line minimum is low): false alarms against the answer key, which is why the thresholds are placeholders for the lead.
- Upstream tags: on the rate-cut slice 485.40 of 697.00 shortfall was not flagged by any upstream agent (an underpayment is not something A1-A5 can see); A3, A5 and A1 flagged the denials mixed into the same slice before billing.
- Ledger, detected 2026-08-31, paid by 2026-10-14: 36,008.24 detected, **0.00 later paid**. The simulator has no appeal, resubmission or re-billing behaviour, so a zero is correct and says nothing about real recovery rates; the ledger arithmetic is tested on a hand-built book.

The planted effects and the agent's windows were written by the same team: this shows the variance and cause arithmetic are wired and exact, not that the thresholds suit a real practice's payer mix or seasonality.

**Known gaps (M21):** variance is realisation against contract only: no volume or payer-mix decomposition, no seasonality model, no location or provider slices. Cause is attribution by key match to leaks A15-lite already detects, not a causal proof; "unexplained" is reported, not guessed. Upstream tagging uses decision records (A1-A5, A13) and matches claims to appointments and visits by payer, patient, date of service and CPT. The ledger counts later payment, not work done or incremental recovery. A9's and A10's proposed patterns are not yet named on the review card. No model is used. The engine gaps (kill switch, maker-checker, versioned policy, identity resolution, before/after values) still stand before any agent leaves shadow.

## 15. Outcome attribution method and financial reporting rule

Distinguish detected, worked, submitted, accepted, paid and incremental recovered; incrementality only against a control (strategy Section 4). **Built (M21): the ledger** (`investigate ledger --detected-on D --through E`) reports **detected at D** and **later paid by E** on the same claim, by finding type and status. It does not claim "worked" (no work log) or "incremental" (no control group): a claim the payer paid on its own after D counts as later paid. Financial reporting rule for this agent: TBD.
