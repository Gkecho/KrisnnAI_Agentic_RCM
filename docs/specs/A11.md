# A11 Payment Posting & Reconciliation Agent

- **Phase:** MVP+; **release:** Wave 3 (A9, A2, A11); **milestone:** M17
- **Build status:** Built in M17, shadow mode only (`krisnai/agents/a11_cash/`, `krisnai a11 sweep|replay`). Reads two new feeds, bank deposits (`bank_deposit`) and PM postings (`posting`), migration 0012. No posting, no bank access, no write-back. M3 already matches remits to claims and checks each payment (BPR02) against its remits.
- **Spec status:** Draft on the strategy's Appendix A (24 fields). Fields the strategy does not state are marked TBD for the owners named in the strategy; thresholds stay TBD until fixed from baseline data before shadow results are reviewed.
- **Engine support (built, M6):** policy table (default allowed, L0, shadow), decision records, idempotency, point-in-time replay, approval queue service, disabled/stub adapters.

## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Days to post; unapplied cash; reconciliation variance.

## 2. Trigger event(s)

ERA/EOB/lockbox/bank transaction received.

## 3. Required data and source systems

835/EOB, lockbox and bank deposit feed (a wave gate; not in the repo).

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Plain code in `rules.reconcile`, one payer payment (835 BPR/TRN) at a time, as of a date. Placeholders for sign-off:
- `deposit_missing`: no deposit 5 days after the payment date. `deposit_variance`: the deposit differs from the payment. `duplicate_deposit`: more than one deposit for it.
- `not_posted`: a paid claim with no posting 3 days after the payment date. `partial_posting`: posted for less than paid (unapplied cash).
- `deposit_late` (over 7 days) and `posting_late` (over 7 days) are KPIs, not work for a person.
- A deposit is matched by trace number; with no trace, by payer + amount within 30 days, one deposit to one payment in date order (equal payments from one payer
  take the deposits in turn). A deposit no payment explains is raised after 7 days. A zero-dollar remittance has no cash to reconcile.
Unapplied cash = not posted + partially posted amounts; reconciliation variance = missing + short + duplicate amounts.

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

Matches payment to claim/account, prepares posting, reconciles ERA to bank deposit, detects unapplied cash/credit balances, identifies variance. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

## 13. Shadow-mode evaluation dataset and acceptance criteria

Dataset and acceptance criteria are fixed before shadow results are reviewed: TBD at spec review.

**Real Synthea book (2026-10-14, 300 patients, seed 7, `--cash`):** 5,782 events. Planted and found, exactly (counts and dollars): 33 missing deposits (3,992.73),
34 short deposits (316.74), 20 duplicates (2,630.02), 44 claims never posted (4,976.71), 31 partly posted (656.49), 25 unidentified deposits (8,093.69); 80 late postings
found. Late deposits: 52 found against 49 planted, the 3 extra caused by payments with equal payer and amount whose untraced deposits cannot be told apart. Unapplied cash
5,633.20, variance 6,939.49; median 2 days to deposit, 1 day to post; 0 executed. Finding on the way: crediting every equal-amount untraced deposit to every equal-amount
payment produced false duplicates, so deposits are now assigned one to one. The feeds and defects are simulated by the same team that wrote the rules.
