# A10 Contract & Underpayment Agent

- **Phase:** MVP; **release:** Wave 2 (A1, A3, A10); **milestone:** M12
- **Build status:** Built in M12, shadow mode only (`krisnnai/agents/a10_underpay/`, `krisnnai a10 replay|patterns`). One decision per paid 835 claim payment; reuses A15-lite's fee-schedule pricer. Contract document extraction, real contracts and dispute submission are not built.
- **Spec status:** Draft on the strategy's Appendix A (24 fields). Fields the strategy does not state are marked TBD for the owners named in the strategy; thresholds stay TBD until fixed from baseline data before shadow results are reviewed.
- **Engine support (built, M6):** policy table (default allowed, L0, shadow), decision records, idempotency, point-in-time replay, approval queue service, disabled/stub adapters.

## 1. Business outcome and KPI

Underpaid $ detected/recovered.

## 2. Trigger event(s)

ERA/EOB received, or a contract updated.

## 3. Required data and source systems

835/EOB, fee schedules and contracts (a synthetic fee schedule exists; real contracts do not), claim history. Falls back to payer-history expected allowed amounts if reliable fee schedules are missing when the Days 31-60 shadow window closes.

## 4. Deterministic rules and payer/client policy inputs

Plain code, no model. For each paid line of an 835 claim payment (reversals and denials are A8's):
`gap = contract allowed (fee schedule x units) - (paid + patient share on the line)`. Rules: `claim_matched`, `short_paid` (gap at or above $1.00 on
fee-schedule pricing; at or above 20% of the expected amount when only payer history prices the line), `contract_priced` (every short line has a fee-schedule row),
`appeal_window_open` (payer window from `payer_appeal_window`, default 180 days). Fee rows are used only when effective on the service date.

Likely cause, from the CARC on the short line (placeholder map in `config.py`, for finance sign-off): only CARC 45 -> contract_pricing; 4 -> modifiers; 59, 97 ->
bundling_ncci; 151 -> units_charges; 50 -> medical_necessity; 197 -> authorization; 29 -> timely_filing; B15, N130 -> payer_policy; 16 or anything unknown ->
data_coding (lower confidence). These are the nine root causes in the source RCM document.

Patterns: short lines for one payer, CPT and cause are bucketed by gap % of contract; the commonest gap and its neighbours (within one point) form a pattern when
there are at least 5 of them and they are at least half of the short lines for that code. One-off short payments on the same code are counted separately. A pattern
is reported as a likely rate change or stale fee-schedule row, with one dispute package for the pattern.

## 5. AI task(s) and approved model(s)

Extracting fee schedule/terms from contract documents. No model is approved yet; the engine's stub provider is the default and a real provider needs an approved BAA (strategy Decision 6).

## 6. Action options and prohibited actions

**Built actions (tasks and reviews only):** `a10_dispute_review` (owner finance; a dispute package on the contract: payer, claim, short lines with allowed / paid /
patient share / gap, cause, deadline, draft letter from the stub model using codes and dollars only) and `a10_contract_check_task` (owner finance; no fee-schedule row,
so confirm the rate before disputing; expected value 0). Nothing is submitted, written off or changed. Original text: Extracts fee schedule/terms, calculates expected allowed and patient share, flags underpayments/incorrect adjustments, prepares a payer dispute package. Prohibited for every agent: any change to CPT, ICD-10 or modifiers, any write-off or refund, and any submission to a payer without a named human approval; submission only through the partner clearinghouse adapter; PM/EHR write-back limited to tasks and notes until each action type is approved under strategy Decision 8.

## 13. Shadow-mode evaluation dataset and acceptance criteria

Dataset and acceptance criteria are fixed before shadow results are reviewed: TBD at spec review.

**Replay on the Synthea book (2026-10-14, 300 patients, seed 7, 18 months, 1,776 claims, a planted 12% rate cut on SYNMEDICARE 99213 from 2026-06-01):** 1,335 paid
payments checked, 166 short-paid for 3,880.53, all dispute reviews, 0 executed. The 166 are exactly the planted 142 one-off underpayments plus the 24 rate-cut lines;
A10 and the A15-lite diagnostic agree to the cent. One pattern for the rate cut (24 lines, 12.0%, 24% of that payer's paid 99213 lines). With the pattern minimum at
3 lines a second, spurious pattern appeared from three unrelated one-offs, so the minimum is 5. The planted problems and the rules share a design, so this shows the
wiring and arithmetic are right, not that the rules would find the right problems in a real practice.
